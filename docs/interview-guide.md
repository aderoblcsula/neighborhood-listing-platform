# Stakeholder Interview Guide

## Discovery Status

These questions are intended for a simulated interview unless the
instructor approves contact with a real participant. No outreach will
occur without instructor and participant approval.

## ChatGPT Prompt

Generate 12 neutral discovery questions for two stakeholder groups:
property professionals and local sponsors using a neighborhood property
listing platform. Cover current workflow, audience, required content,
content approval, ownership and access, inquiries, success measures,
and accessibility. Avoid leading questions, requests for sensitive
personal information, and assumptions that a proposed feature is useful.
The questions should help identify candidate product requirements and
access rules for a later database implementation.

## Candidate Questions Generated by ChatGPT

1. How do you currently create, review, and update property or sponsor
   content?

2. What information is necessary before a property listing or sponsor
   submission is ready for review?

3. Who is the intended audience for your content, and what information
   does that audience need?

4. Who should be permitted to create, edit, review, approve, publish,
   or remove content?

5. What steps should content pass through between its initial creation
   and publication?

6. What should happen when submitted content is rejected or requires
   corrections?

7. Which information should be publicly visible, and which information
   should remain restricted?

8. How should the platform handle questions or inquiries submitted by
   visitors?

9. Who should be allowed to view and respond to visitor inquiries?

10. What measures would help determine whether the platform is meeting
    your needs?

11. What accessibility or device-related needs should the platform
    support?

12. What concerns would you have about submitting, storing, or displaying
    information through this platform?

## Selection Status

These questions are candidates only. They must be reviewed by Gemini
for leading language and privacy risks before the final eight questions
are selected.

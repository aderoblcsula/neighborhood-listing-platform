# Stakeholder Facts, Assumptions, and Uncertainties

## Evidence Notice

The Excel occupational task list is used only as a general reference
for property-professional workflows. It does not represent an interview
with an actual stakeholder.

## Property Professionals

### Known Facts

- Property professionals promote properties through advertisements,
  open houses, and listing services.
- They compile and review property listings.
- They explain property features to prospective clients.
- They compare properties to help determine competitive market prices.
- They identify properties that match buyers' needs.
- The course project requires property content to follow an approval
  workflow.

### Assumptions Requiring Confirmation

- Property professionals can create their own listing records.
- Property professionals can edit their own unapproved listings.
- Property professionals cannot approve their own listings.
- Rejected listings can be corrected and resubmitted.
- Visitors can only view approved listings.
- An administrator grants final publication approval.

## Local Sponsors

### Known Facts

- Local sponsors are one of the platform's required stakeholder groups.
- Sponsor content and claims require a documented approval workflow.
- Sponsored content must be distinguishable from property information.

### Assumptions Requiring Confirmation

- Sponsors can create and edit their own unapproved records.
- Sponsor placements require administrator approval.
- Sponsors cannot approve their own placements.
- Sponsors cannot modify another sponsor's records.
- Rejected sponsor content can be corrected and resubmitted.
- Only approved and active sponsor content is publicly visible.

## Platform Access Uncertainties

1. Can sponsors manage their own records?
2. Must every sponsor placement receive approval?
3. Can visitors submit inquiries without signing in?
4. Who can read submitted inquiries?
5. Can listing owners create listings but not approve them?
6. Can owners edit and resubmit rejected listings?
7. Which property fields should be publicly visible?
8. Which actions require administrator approval?

## Preliminary Working Rules

The following rules are proposals rather than confirmed requirements:

- Public visitors may read only approved listings and active,
  approved sponsor content.
- Authenticated owners may create and edit only their own unapproved
  or rejected content.
- Owners may not approve their own content.
- Administrators perform final approval through secure server-side
  authorization.
- Inquiry access should be limited to authorized recipients and
  administrators.
- Inquiry forms should collect only the information necessary to
  respond.

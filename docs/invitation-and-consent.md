# Stakeholder Discovery Invitation and Consent Notice

**Course:** CIS 3610  
**Institution:** California State University, Los Angeles  
**Student:** Aaron De Robles

## Simulation Notice

This document is an ethical outreach template for a hypothetical
stakeholder discovery exercise. It was not sent to a real participant,
and no actual stakeholder outreach occurred.

## Purpose

Hello. I am a student in CIS 3610 at California State University,
Los Angeles. As part of a course assignment, I am conducting a brief
practice stakeholder discovery interview about workflows and challenges
related to a neighborhood property-listing platform.

This conversation is for academic discovery only. It is not a sales,
advertising, or marketing request.

## Time Commitment

The conversation would take approximately 15–20 minutes.

## Voluntary Participation

Participation is entirely voluntary. A participant may:

- Decline to participate
- Skip any question
- Stop the conversation at any time
- Withdraw without penalty or consequence

## Data Use and Privacy

Only minimal written notes would be collected. The notes would be
de-identified, summarized, and used only for academic analysis within
the course project.

De-identified notes may be analyzed using approved AI tools to identify
themes and candidate requirements. AI-generated summaries would be
checked against the original notes before being accepted.

Participants should not provide sensitive personal information,
customer information, account credentials, financial or credit records,
confidential business information, or proprietary business secrets.

No audio or video recording would occur without separate, explicit
permission.

## Consent Questions

Before beginning an approved real interview, the student would ask:

1. Do you understand the purpose and expected 15–20 minute duration?
2. Do you voluntarily agree to participate?
3. Do you give permission for minimal written notes to be taken?
4. Do you understand that you may skip questions or stop at any time?
5. Do you understand that de-identified notes may be analyzed with an
   AI tool for this academic exercise?

The interview would continue only after the participant answered yes
to the applicable consent questions.

## Simulation Status

For the current lab, the stakeholder interviews are AI-generated
simulations. No real person is being interviewed. The simulated
responses must be labeled as synthetic and cannot be presented as
validated statements from actual stakeholders.

# Evidence-Based Working Personas

## Evidence Status

These personas are working hypotheses derived from AI-generated simulated
interviews. No real property professionals or local businesses were contacted.
The personas describe operational needs rather than verified characteristics of
real stakeholder populations. All findings require stakeholder confirmation
before becoming final product requirements.
## Persona 1: Property Professional

**Archetype:** Property listing coordinator  
**Evidence base:** SIM-PROP-01 through SIM-PROP-03

### Primary Responsibilities

- Create and maintain accurate property listings.
- Update pricing, availability, amenities, policies, and property details.
- Correct rejected submissions and keep published information current.
- Review and respond to visitor inquiries when authorized.

### Current Workflow

- Listing information is prepared through individual templates, spreadsheets,
  or property-management records.
- Content passes through internal or platform review before publication.
- Rejected content is corrected and resubmitted.
- Inquiries route either to an assigned agent or a shared leasing-office queue.

### Operational Pain Points

- Repeating listing updates across multiple locations.
- Receiving unclear explanations when a submission is rejected.
- Managing frequent availability changes.
- Separating public listing content from private operational information.
- Ensuring that inquiries reach the correct person or team.

### Functional Goals

- Create and update listings through a centralized interface.
- Receive clear correction instructions for rejected listings.
- Publish only reviewed and approved information.
- Manage inquiries through an appropriate individual or shared workflow.
- Use the platform effectively on mobile and desktop devices.

### Access and Approval Needs

- Property personnel need access to their own listing records.
- Authorized reviewers must control final publication.
- Rejected listings should remain editable by their authorized owners.
- Private notes, tenant information, lease records, and internal instructions
  must not appear in public views.
- Inquiry access should be limited to assigned personnel and administrators.

### Definition of Success

- Public listings remain accurate and current.
- Unapproved records are not publicly visible.
- Corrections can be made without recreating the listing.
- Inquiries reach the correct agent or leasing team.
- Public and restricted information remain appropriately separated.

### Uncertainties Requiring Confirmation

- Whether owners may approve their own listings.
- Whether minor published changes require renewed approval.
- Whether rejected listings retain revision history.
- Whether unauthenticated visitors may submit inquiries.
- Which personnel may read and respond to inquiries.
- Whether bulk property updates belong in the Week 8 MVP.

## Persona 2: Local Sponsor

**Archetype:** Local business content coordinator  
**Evidence base:** SIM-SPON-01 through SIM-SPON-03

### Primary Responsibilities

- Maintain accurate public business information.
- Prepare promotional content for review.
- Correct rejected or outdated submissions.
- Monitor aggregate placement performance.

### Current Workflow

- Promotional information is prepared internally and approved by business
  personnel before external publication.
- Updates are manually repeated across directories, social platforms, community
  pages, or printed materials.
- Calendar reminders are commonly used to manage placement expiration.

### Operational Pain Points

- Re-entering or manually republishing the same information.
- Unclear reasons for content rejection.
- Keeping temporary promotions and operating information current.
- Maintaining separation between public information and private business data.

### Functional Goals

- Create and revise sponsor profiles through a centralized interface.
- Receive specific correction information when content is rejected.
- Publish accessible content that works on mobile and desktop devices.
- Review aggregate engagement measures without unnecessary personal tracking.

### Access and Approval Needs

- Sponsors need permission to manage their own submitted records.
- Public publication and promotional claims may require administrative review.
- Private operational, financial, supplier, and employee information must not
  appear in public views.

### Definition of Success

- Accurate sponsor information is published through a clear review process.
- Corrections can be submitted without recreating the entire record.
- Expired promotions are removed or flagged reliably.
- Reporting provides useful aggregate measures while minimizing visitor data.

### Uncertainties Requiring Confirmation

- Whether all sponsor changes require approval.
- Whether sponsors may edit already-published records directly.
- Which promotional claims and credentials require verification.
- Whether placement occurs near listings, within directories, or both.
- Which sponsor-specific fields belong in the Week 8 MVP.

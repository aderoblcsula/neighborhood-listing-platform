## Recommended Strongest Eight Neutral Questions

To build an efficient, comprehensive research instrument that covers all required domains (workflow, audience, content approval, success measures, accessibility, and data access) without redundancy or cognitive overload, use the following eight finalized questions:

1. **Workflow:** How do you currently create, review, and update property or sponsor content, and what steps should it pass through between initial creation and publication?
2. **Content Requirements:** What information is necessary before a property listing or sponsor submission is ready for review?
3. **Audience & Content:** Who is the intended audience for your content, and what information does that audience need?
4. **Approval & Permissions:** Which roles or permissions should govern who can create, approve, publish, or remove content?
5. **Data Access & Privacy:** Which information should be publicly visible, and what data should remain restricted or protected?
6. **Inquiry Management:** How should visitor inquiries be handled, and who should be allowed to view and respond to them?
7. **Success Measures:** What measures or criteria would help determine whether the platform is successfully meeting your needs?
8. **Accessibility & Technical Needs:** What accessibility, device-related support, or operational concerns should be factored into the platform design?

## Exact Prompt

Create three synthetic local-sponsor stakeholder interviews for a
CIS 3610 academic discovery exercise involving a neighborhood
property-listing platform.

Use these simulated operational roles:

- SIM-SPON-01: Neighborhood café or restaurant manager
- SIM-SPON-02: Local home-services provider
- SIM-SPON-03: Independent neighborhood retailer

Important rules:

1. Clearly label every stakeholder and response as simulated.
2. Answer the same eight questions for each role.
3. Produce exactly 24 responses: eight responses for each of the three
   stakeholders.
4. Keep each Simulated Response between two and four sentences.
5. Focus only on current promotional workflows, operational
   responsibilities, content approval, public information, placement,
   success measures, and accessibility.
6. Do not invent personal names, ages, income, hobbies, family details,
   protected characteristics, customer records, confidential business
   information, or fictional personal histories.
7. Do not claim that these responses came from real interviews.
8. Describe current practices separately from desired changes.
9. Allow the three simulated roles to have different or conflicting
   workflows and priorities.
10. Do not assume that every proposed platform feature is useful.
11. Do not invent laws, legal obligations, advertising rules, pricing
    models, fees, contracts, or university policies.
12. Do not claim that a promotional statement is truthful or verified.
13. If a stakeholder would not control or know an answer, state that
    confirmation is required from a platform administrator, property
    professional, content reviewer, or other appropriate role.
14. Treat all proposed functions as candidate needs rather than
    confirmed requirements.
15. Do not interpret repeated AI-generated answers as stakeholder
    consensus.
